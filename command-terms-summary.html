Command Terms Summary Table

Every command term verified in the written-response sections of the 2020–2025 papers, with mark range, frequency, and the exact expectation statement used under each term in the guide.

Reference: VCAA Glossary of command terms

Command termMarks seenItems 20–25What the question is really askingExact expectation statement
Identify1–4~35Name the correct thing. No elaboration unless a second verb asks for it.Name or recognise the correct thing from your knowledge or from the material provided.
State1–4~18A short, correct answer. One mark per distinct point.Give a brief, correct answer without working or elaboration.
Describe1–4~30Give the features or characteristics of something, in context.Give an account of the features or characteristics of something, or of how something happens.
Explain2–6~33Say how or why something works, with the causal link made visible.Make the reasons, mechanism or relationship clear — say how or why, not just what.
Justify2–416Choose one option, argue for it, and argue against the alternative and link to the stimulus.Show, prove or defend a decision or point of view with reasoning and evidence, including why the alternative is less suitable.
Complete2–7~18Fill in a diagram, test table, data dictionary or pseudocode correctly.Fill in the missing parts of a supplied diagram, table, data dictionary or algorithm so that it is correct and consistent.
Suggest1–4~14Offer a workable course of action that fits the scenario.Put forward a workable idea or course of action for consideration.
Outline2–46Set out the main points briefly — more than State, less than Describe.Set out the main points or features briefly, without full explanation.
Discuss2–33Work through the implications of an issue, not just name it.Examine an issue by considering its implications, and reach a reasoned position on it.
Compare22Set two things against each other on the same criterion.Set two things side by side and identify how they differ, using the same basis for each.
Classify / Categorise2–45Sort given items into given categories. Usually a table.Assign each given item to the correct category from a supplied list.
Select / Circle / Choose1–4~14Pick from given options. Usually 1 mark, usually followed by a second verb.Pick the correct option or options from those provided.
Recommend2–44Advise a specific action. Always paired with a second verb.Advise a specific course of action as the best one available.
Propose2–34Put forward a plan or strategy, usually with named components.Put forward a plan, strategy or approach for a stated purpose.
List23Items only. No sentences required.Give the required number of items, one per line, without elaboration.
Name11The exact term, nothing else.Give the exact term only.
Define11The study-design meaning of the term.Give the study-design meaning.
Construct21Build a complete set — here, a set of tests.Produce the requested artefact with all stated parts.
Determine1–22Work it out and commit to an answer.Make a decision using the given data.
Calculate11A numeric answer, in the units asked for.Provide the correct numeric result in the requested unit.
Write1–4~8Produce the artefact itself — for example, a line of pseudocode or a label.Provide the exact required text/notation.
Rewrite13Produce the corrected artefact itself — for example, the full corrected line of pseudocode.Provide the full corrected line/text exactly.
Draw11Produce the artefact itself — for example, an association on a diagram.Place the correct diagram element in the correct position.
